Novabia / Companions

COMPANIONS & EVOLUTION

Three generations. Playable and non-playable companions.

Novabia includes companion families that can join the player's journey, as well as non-playable companions that help define the world. Below is the current Gen 1 showcase: Hound, Crab, and Spore.

3 Generations planned

Companion growth is designed across three gens, giving each family room to evolve visually and strategically.

2 Companion roles

Novabia features both playable companions and non-playable companions that populate the wider world.

Gen 1 Current showcase

The first featured companion families are Hound, Crab, and Spore.

GEN 1 FAMILY

Hound

A flagship early companion line showing multiple routes, instincts, and elemental outcomes.

GEN 1 FAMILY

Crab

A second Gen 1 family built around branching crab evolutions and contrasting elemental outcomes.

GEN 1 FAMILY

Spore

The Spore family begins with Floro, Alpine, and Umbral basic forms, reaches a shared Phantom stage, then branches into powerful fusion and venomous forms.

Evolution follows the journey: a Stage 3 companion can preserve the Heir's identity while a Tutor from a different provincial branch contributes learned adaptation. This is a design explanation, not a claim that all planned evolutions are implemented in the present build.
