THE WORLD

A continent changed by another world.

Novabia takes place across a fractured continent of four provinces. At its center lies the Crossroads — a fortified checkpoint built after a multidimensional port brought refugees, conflict, and powerful untamed companions into the world.

01

Explore

Travel through provinces shaped by different cultures, dangers, factions, and ecological pressures.

02

Care

Build and manage a companion team while treating, transporting, and protecting creatures encountered on the road.

03

Evolve

Evolution is not one fixed ladder. Species biology and elemental catalysts can open different developmental routes.

COMPANION EVOLUTION

The same catalyst does not create the same creature.

White crystals act as biological catalysts. Their effects depend on the species they touch, making evolution a relationship between creature biology and elemental influence rather than a universal, predetermined upgrade tree.

F

Floro

Associated with photosynthetic life and plant-linked biology.

P

Pyro

Interacts with warm-blooded creatures and heat-linked traits.

W

Water

Influences cold-adapted, aquatic, and shoreline creatures.
