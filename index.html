THE WORLD

A continent built for adventure.

Novabia spans volcanic frontiers, snowy forests, sacred highlands, industrial cities, mystical woods, and ancient lakes. Its journey structure is built around 37 different roads that connect crossroads, provinces, and societies with radically different values.

This world is designed not just as a game setting, but as the foundation of the broader Novabia entertainment IP — a continent where each road can deliver its own encounters, rewards, and story beats.

3 Core origins
4+ Distinct regions
37 Different roads

THREE ORIGINS

Begin from a different life.

Each path introduces a different worldview, skillset, and relationship with the world around you.

ELEMENTAL / CARETAKER

Field Clinic Route

Travel as a young healer and companion caretaker. Learn the rhythms of survival, treatment, recovery, and crystal-linked transformation.

KINETIC / DOJO

Warrior Route

Enter a path of discipline, combat, and rising challenge. Train strength, test conviction, and face rivals through battles that reveal hidden tensions beneath the surface.

LANDSCAPE / MONASTIC

Highland Route

Walk a more reflective road through mountains and sacred spaces, uncovering wisdom, history, and balance between nature, belief, and power.

White Crystals

Consume The White Crystals, and you may Suffer or unlock Elemental power.

Honor Medals

Fight your way up and get ranked based on how many Honor Medals collected.

Nature Essence

Stand High On Top of the Icy Mountains and gather the Nature Essence.
