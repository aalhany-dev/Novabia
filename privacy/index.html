1. Introduction

Welcome to Novabia — Legend Evolution ("Novabia", "the Game", "we", "us", or "our").

Novabia is developed and operated by Hany Ali, an independent developer based in Egypt, operating under the name Novabia. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and protected, and how you can exercise your privacy rights. It applies to Novabia's gameplay services, cloud-linked accounts, official website, playtest registration, and related support communications.

Our commitment: We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide our services. We do not sell your personal information or use your gameplay data for targeted advertising.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign in using a supported third-party authentication provider, such as Google or Facebook, our authentication service may receive and maintain:

  • A unique user identifier (UID).
  • Your display name, if provided.
  • Your email address, if made available by the provider.
  • Your profile photo URL, if provided.
  • Authentication-related information necessary to maintain your account.

This information is provided through the authentication provider and managed using Google Firebase Authentication. We do not request or store your Google or Facebook account password.

2.2 Game Save Data

When cloud saving is enabled for your account, we store gameplay information associated with your UID. This information may include:

  • Game progress, completed challenges, and achievements.
  • Characters, creatures, and their attributes.
  • Inventory, collected items, and in-game currencies.
  • Player progression and other information necessary to restore your game state.

This information is used to maintain your progress and, where supported, synchronize it across devices.

2.3 Guest Gameplay

Novabia offers guest gameplay without requiring a third-party account. Guest progress is stored locally on your device or within your browser's local storage, depending on the platform. Guest save data is not linked to a cloud account unless a supported transfer or synchronization feature is used. Clearing browser storage, removing application data, or uninstalling the game may permanently erase locally stored progress. Although guest gameplay does not require an authentication account, website hosting and other technical service providers may still process limited technical information necessary to deliver the service.

2.4 Technical Information

Our infrastructure and service providers may automatically process technical information, including:

  • IP addresses.
  • Browser or application information.
  • Device and operating-system information.
  • User-agent strings.
  • Authentication events and related security records.
  • Technical information necessary for service operation and abuse prevention.

This information is used to maintain functionality, protect accounts, and support the security and reliability of Novabia.

2.5 Playtest Registration

Novabia may invite players to participate in optional playtesting through a separate registration form linked from our official website. Depending on the information requested in the form, we may collect:

  • Your name or preferred display name.
  • Your email address.
  • Gaming interests and preferences.
  • Device or platform information.
  • Answers to questions about your gameplay experience.
  • Feedback voluntarily submitted during testing.

We use this information to evaluate applications, communicate with participants, coordinate playtesting, and improve the Game. Playtest registration information is not automatically linked to your cloud-linked game account unless this is separately explained. If an external form provider is used, that provider may process information according to its own privacy practices. Registering for playtesting does not automatically subscribe you to promotional newsletters or unrelated marketing communications.

2.6 Support Communications

When you contact us, we may receive your email address, message, and any additional information you voluntarily provide. We use this information to respond to inquiries, investigate technical problems, process privacy requests, and maintain relevant support records. Support emails sent to our official domain may be processed through Cloudflare Email Routing and forwarded to our designated support mailbox.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for legitimate and disclosed purposes, including:

  • Authentication: Identifying and authenticating players who choose cloud-linked accounts.
  • Cloud Saving: Storing, recovering, and synchronizing gameplay progress.
  • Service Operation: Maintaining the functionality, availability, and security of the Game.
  • Account Protection: Preventing unauthorized access, abuse, and fraudulent activity.
  • Playtesting: Organizing voluntary tests, communicating with participants, and evaluating feedback.
  • Support: Responding to technical questions and privacy requests.
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting applicable legal obligations and responding to valid legal requests.

Where applicable law requires consent, we rely on the appropriate consent for the relevant processing activity. Other processing may be necessary to provide a service you request, meet legal obligations, or protect the legitimate operation and security of the service, where permitted by law. We do not sell or rent personal information to advertisers or data brokers. We do not use cloud save data to create targeted advertising profiles.

5. Local Storage, Cookies, and Authentication Sessions

Novabia and its service providers may use local storage, session information, authentication tokens, and, where applicable, cookies. These technologies may be used to:

  • Maintain authenticated sessions.
  • Remember essential session information.
  • Preserve local gameplay progress.
  • Support essential security and service functions.

Authentication tokens and local storage may remain on a device after closing the browser, depending on the authentication configuration. You may manage local browser data using your device or browser settings. However, deleting this information may sign you out of your account or permanently erase guest gameplay progress.

6. Children's Privacy

Novabia is designed to provide an enjoyable gaming experience for a broad audience, including younger players where its content is age-appropriate. However, the suitability of game content for children does not automatically make children eligible to create online accounts or submit personal information. Until appropriate parental-consent mechanisms are implemented, independent cloud-account registration and playtest registration are intended for individuals aged 18 or older. Younger players may enjoy guest gameplay, where available, with appropriate parental or guardian supervision. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through independent account registration without the permissions required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without the required authorization, please contact us at support@novabia.dev so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

7. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell players' personal information. We may share information in limited circumstances:

  • With service providers necessary to operate the Game.
  • With authentication providers selected by the player.
  • With playtest service providers necessary to process voluntary registration.
  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • When reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect service security, or protect the rights and safety of others, subject to applicable law.

We do not publicly disclose individual players' cloud save data.

8. International Data Processing

Novabia is operated from Egypt, but some of our technology providers operate internationally. As a result, authentication information, cloud data, support communications, or other service information may be processed or stored outside Egypt, including in the United States and other countries where relevant providers operate. International processing may be subject to different data protection laws and safeguards. We seek to address applicable requirements governing international data processing and transfers.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose or as required by applicable law. In general:

  • Account information: Retained while your cloud-linked account remains active, subject to deletion requests and applicable requirements.
  • Cloud save data: Retained to maintain your game progress until deletion, account closure, or another valid reason for removal.
  • Guest save data: Retained locally until removed by the player, the browser, the application, or the device.
  • Playtest registration: Retained for the period reasonably necessary to organize playtesting, evaluate feedback, and complete related communications.
  • Support requests: Retained as necessary to resolve requests, maintain appropriate records, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Technical records: Retained according to operational needs and the relevant providers' retention procedures.

Some deleted information may remain temporarily in secure backups or technical records until the applicable deletion or retention cycle is complete.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information associated with you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about how your personal information is processed.
  • Exercise other rights available under applicable data protection laws.

To make a request, contact: support@novabia.dev

We may request reasonable information to verify that you are authorized to access or delete the relevant account. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Novabia cloud-linked account and associated gameplay data at any time. Send your request to: support@novabia.dev

Please include your display name and UID, if available. We may request additional verification to prevent unauthorized deletion of another player's account. After verifying a valid request, we will arrange deletion of the associated authentication account and cloud save records, subject to applicable legal and technical retention requirements. Deletion may be permanent and may prevent future recovery of game progress. Deleting your Novabia account does not delete your Google or Facebook account. Locally stored guest data must generally be removed directly from your browser or device.

12. Data Security

We use established third-party infrastructure and seek to apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. However, no internet-based service, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Players should protect access to their devices and third-party authentication accounts.

If you suspect unauthorized access or a security issue affecting your Novabia account, please contact us.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Game, our technologies, applicable legal requirements, or our data processing practices. The latest version will be published on our official website with an updated revision date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain appropriate consent before implementing material changes in how personal information is processed.

14. Regulatory Rights

You may have the right to lodge a complaint with an applicable data protection authority. For matters subject to Egyptian data protection law, information about the Personal Data Protection Center is available at: https://pdpc.gov.eg/
